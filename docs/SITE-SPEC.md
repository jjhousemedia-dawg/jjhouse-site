## 0. What this is not

Front-loaded because this is where sessions have gone wrong.

The site is **not** the first-person camera-rig concept — POV hands holding a camera, framing a 3D
house on a dune, shot-list post-it, four role routes. That was the previous version. It is dead.
Its code is quarantined in `_deprecated/`. Nothing from it carries over.

The site is **not** sorted by job title. `/director`, `/producer`, `/vfx-edit`, `/for-fun` are gone
and must not come back in any form. **Splitting the work by which hat JJ wore is the exact problem
this rebuild exists to solve.**

The site is **not** a gallery or a grid. jjhouse.me already is one, and it is what we are moving
away from.

The copy is **not** written in studio-site voice. See §4.

## 2. The thesis

> Things change on the way to getting made. Some of that change is the piece working out what it
> actually is, and some of it is attrition. From the inside they look identical. Telling them
> apart, early enough to act, is most of the job.

A **judgment** claim, not a reliability claim. An earlier draft built on keeping promises was
rejected by JJ because preventing change also prevents improvement, and rigid reads worse than
drifty. The skill was never holding the line; it is knowing when the line should move.

Underlying test, JJ's own words, predating this project: *"is this actually becoming what it's
trying to be?"*

His stated superpower, from his LMU MELM program: **framing ideas and presenting them** so a room
gets it. Lead with strategy and framing; production and budget are credibility, not identity.

## 4. Copy

**Register.** JJ's real voice: flowing sentences with commas, load-bearing analogies, dry
parentheticals, honest uncertainty. Deliberately **not** the ad-fragment register every studio site
uses. Note that every noth.in headline is an "it's not X, it's Y" parallel, and that construction is
on JJ's banned list — the differentiation here is that a person wrote it.

**Banned:** em-dashes · "it's not X, it's Y" · fragments for punch · tricolons as rhythm · "here's
the thing" · "it's important to note" · academic throat-clearing · empty closers. Style guide:
`JJ_House_Writing_Style_Guide.md` on the Mac.

**Reliable tell:** when copy fails on this project, it went abstract. Stay concrete.

## 5. Mechanics

### Loader — not yet built
Always inward. No vector ever points outward; material arrives from offscreen, converges, replaces
what was there. Consequence: material is *added* each beat, so the egg stays buried under everything
and the final mark contains every shape before it.

```
nothing → shudder → shrapnel storms in and locks   → EGG (always first)
        → shudder → crumbles inward as new material
                    arrives through and past it     → OBJECT 2 (random)
        → shudder → same again                      → OBJECT 3 (random)
        → final convergence                         → THE MARK
```

Absorb, don't cover — nesting is felt, not literal, which is what frees the order. One gravitational
centre. Flat matte silhouettes, never reads as 3D. Every assembly is a **reverse-played fracture
sim**. **Outgoing material must be finer than incoming** — dust out, chunks in; matched grain gives
six frames of mush and that contrast is the whole illusion. The shudder is the anticipation frame
that makes the object appear to decide. ~2s total.

### Hero — not yet built
**Surface minimal, substrate maximal.** Restraint on top means the interaction has somewhere to go,
and the wordmark still works as a static screenshot.

| Layer | |
|---|---|
| Surface | Flat, near-dead. SVG or 3D rendered to read as 2D. Slow chase or rim light only. Desaturated. |
| Reveal | Blender geo-nodes work — converted-font geometry, hair systems. **In colour.** |

Drag erodes the surface; it refills like foam closing over a lake. Architecture, verified from
noth.in's bundle: wordmark rasterized via OffscreenCanvas → texture; full-screen quad
(PlaneGeometry + OrthographicCamera); ping-pong WebGLRenderTarget accumulating pointer position
weighted by velocity, multiplied by a decay constant each frame; procedural noise added before
thresholding so the erosion edge is cellular. `alpha = matte − threshold(trail + noise)`.

Three numbers are the entire feel: **decay constant ~0.94** (this *is* the foam), stamp radius ~8%
viewport width, noise scale matched to hair scale.

Blender output: **two passes**, beauty and a separate matte (white geo on black). Don't render with
alpha and fight premultiplied edges.

Must handle touch drag, and run a slow autonomous drift on phones so the idea isn't invisible there.

### Reel — placeholder in place
Enters full-bleed, recedes with perspective to a screen at the far end of a rendered room, floor
reflection under it. Sound toggle on entry, off by default. **The flip happens here.**

Build it the way noth.in does: **two pre-rendered MP4s**, a beauty plate and a *separate reflection
pass*, stacked and scroll-scrubbed. Their gallery is not real-time 3D. A weekend in Blender.

Room is `OPEN` — empty theatre, edit bay at 2am, soundstage after wrap.

### Works ticker — built and working
Top-left slot defaults to `WORKS`. When a project enters view it cycles **that project's own
credits**, ~500ms each, hard cuts like burned-in subtitles, looping until it exits. The **rate of
change is the information**: multi-hat projects flicker, single-hat projects sit still. Fixed-width
box or the layout jitters. Rejected: one credit per project, which flattens the interesting thing.

### Objects — not yet built
**Egg · duck decoy · keystone · tuning fork · golf iron.** Dead-centre, symmetrical, unreasonably
serious lighting, black and white. **Never captioned, never explained, no tooltips.**

The duck is load-bearing, not a whim: a decoy is an object built to be believed, and a duck on water
is a composed surface with everything happening underneath — which is the hero mechanic. Keystone is
Pennsylvania, an IYKYK. Rejected: plumb bob (silhouette clash, plus the obvious problem), grey card
(doesn't read), scale rule (too architectural), and noth.in's kitsch set.

## 7. Content commitments

**A making-of anecdote per project.** Promised in the beat 03 copy, so it has to exist.

```
what it was trying to be     one line
what went wrong              one line, dry, no self-pity
the call                     one line — load-bearing
what it bought               one line, result in it
```

40–60 words. **Every anecdote ends on a call JJ made, not on the chaos** — war stories alone read as
someone who attracts disasters; the same stories ending on a decision read as judgment, which is the
site's argument. **Name conditions, never people, as causes.**

Known material: Charli XCX are the headliners · **The Districts**, shot in the LA River, the DoP's
car totalled in Frogtown before a frame rolled, a gross location that photographed beautifully (the
car is loss, the river is discovery — land both halves) · **RÜFÜS "Music is Better" is "basically our
Fitzcarraldo"**, and is the long-version beat. Fitzcarraldo is the diplomatic solve: it moves the
antagonist to the ambition, so everyone comes out looking like they attempted something
disproportionate rather than incompetent. That anecdote is the one place the grand register is
earned; everything else stays dry.

## 8. Standing rule — no invented biography

Nothing about JJ's life, projects, credits or history goes into copy unless he said it or it is in
the verified credit roster. **Where a line needs a detail you don't have, leave a bracket.**

This has gone wrong twice and he caught both: an AD walking off a set (assembled from two real
facts into a scene that never happened), and a description of how "Chaeri" was made.
